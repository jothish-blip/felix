## 2. Objective & Approach

The primary objective is to evaluate the external security posture of [Target Application / Domain], identify actionable vulnerabilities before malicious actors exploit them, and provide your engineering team with reproducible evidence and direct remediation guidance.

### Methodology Highlights
- **Evidence-First Verification:** No generic scanner noise. Every reported finding includes reproducible HTTP requests and response proofs.
- **Safe & Non-Destructive:** Testing is conducted within agreed parameters using read-only techniques, avoiding service interruption.
- **Human-Reviewed Prioritization:** Vulnerabilities are prioritized based on their actual exploitability and business risk to [Client Company Name].
- **Remediation Walkthrough & Retest:** We don't just dump a PDF report; we review findings with your technical team and perform a verification retest after fixes are deployed.

## 4. Deliverables

Upon completion, [Client Company Name] will receive:

1. **Executive Security Summary:** High-level overview of overall risk posture, severity distribution, and strategic recommendations for executive leadership.
2. **Technical Findings & Remediation Report:**
   - Detailed vulnerability descriptions with CVSS 3.1 severity scores.
   - Exact HTTP reproduction steps (ready-to-run `curl` commands).
   - Code-level and architecture-level remediation advice.
3. **Findings Review Call (30–45 Mins):** A direct technical debrief between our lead assessor and your engineering team to discuss fixes.
4. **Remediation Verification & Retest:** Retesting of resolved findings within 30 days of the initial report, followed by a formal **Letter of Attestation**.
